    Google Alerts Gmail connection

    Personal inbox integration for Google Alerts only. Job Beeper reads messages carrying your JobBeeper/GoogleAlerts label. Create the label and Gmail filter yourself; Job Beeper never edits mailbox labels or filters.

    How this data is used: Job Beeper checks message metadata and sender authentication, then temporarily parses eligible message bodies in memory to extract destination links. It does not save raw email bodies, email snippets, alert search queries, subjects or sender addresses. A one-way message-key digest and sync/processing status are retained for deduplication and sync recovery.

    Job Beeper follows each destination link, fetches and verifies the public job posting, and saves public posting fields (such as title, company, location, description and public URL) as a private job discovery in your account. Those records are matched against your Job Beeper preferences and displayed in your dashboard.

    Telegram: if you separately enable Job Beeper Telegram notifications, matching job details and public posting links may be sent to your linked private Telegram chat. Telegram is a separate service. Gmail connection alone does not turn Telegram notifications on.

    Consent record: when you accept, Job Beeper stores your Job Beeper account ID, the disclosure version and the acceptance time as a consent record. It remains until your Job Beeper account is deleted, even after Gmail is disconnected.

    Disconnect and deletion: disconnect revokes the Gmail grant where possible and removes Gmail credentials, sync/message metadata and Gmail-only private discoveries. Pending Gmail historical notifications are cancelled; notification-ledger history may remain. Job records also known independently from public sources are retained.
